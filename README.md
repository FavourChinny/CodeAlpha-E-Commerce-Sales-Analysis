# CodeAlpha-E-Commerce-Sales-Analysis

## Project Overview

This project analyzes e-commerce sales data to uncover insights into sales performance, product categories, customer behavior, delivery performance, and revenue trends.

The analysis was performed using Python and data visualization libraries to transform raw sales data into meaningful business insights that can support data-driven decision-making.

## Objectives

- Analyze overall e-commerce sales performance.
- Identify high-performing product categories.
- Examine revenue trends over time.
- Understand customer purchasing behavior.
- Analyze delivery performance and customer ratings.
- Examine relationships between key variables.
- Generate actionable business recommendations.

## Tools and Technologies

- Python
- Pandas
- NumPy
- Matplotlib
- Seaborn
- Jupyter Notebook

### Correlation Analysis

Correlation analysis was used to examine relationships between:

- Quantity
- Unit price
- Discount
- Revenue
- Customer rating
- Delivery days

## Key Insights

- Revenue shows variations across product categories.
- Unit price and quantity have stronger relationships with revenue than most other variables.
- Discounts show a weak negative relationship with revenue.
- Customer ratings have very little linear relationship with revenue.
- Longer delivery times may be associated with lower average customer ratings at higher delivery durations.

## Business Recommendations

- Focus on high-performing product categories.
- Review underperforming categories for improvement opportunities.
- Optimize discount strategies rather than relying heavily on discounts.
- Monitor delivery performance to improve customer experience.
- Maintain convenient and popular payment options.
- Use customer feedback and ratings to identify areas for improvement.
- Monitor revenue trends to support future sales planning.

## Conclusion

This project demonstrates how Python-based data analysis and visualization can transform raw e-commerce data into actionable business insights.

The findings can help businesses understand sales performance, customer behavior, product performance, and operational trends while supporting data-driven decision-making.
